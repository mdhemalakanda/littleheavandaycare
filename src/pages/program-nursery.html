Nursery

Nursery is where letters and numbers become friends. Through stories, counting games and art, children meet A-B-C and 1-2-3 without any pressure.

Every activity hides a lesson: building blocks teach shapes, role play teaches words, and group time teaches patience and friendship.

  • Letters & numbers through play
  • Storytelling every day
  • Drawing, clay and crafts

Daycare Program

Little Heaven daycare is a family, not a facility. While you work with peace of mind, your child plays, eats, rests and learns under the watchful eyes of caring teachers.

Our welcome reel says it best: a friendly, nurturing, child-focused environment where every child is loved, cared for and encouraged to learn through play.

  • Full-day and half-day options
  • Caring teachers watching over every child
  • Birthdays and special days celebrated together

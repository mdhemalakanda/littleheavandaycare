Kindergarten

In Kindergarten, English starts to flow. Children speak in short sentences, recognise letters and sounds, and begin writing their first words — always with games and encouragement.

Maths becomes discovery: patterns, sizes and counting turn into puzzles and races. Science becomes wonder: plants, animals and seasons through stories and experiments.

  • Phonics and first writing
  • Speaking English with confidence
  • Numbers, patterns and puzzles

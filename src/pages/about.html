Our Story

A happy place to learn & grow

Little Heaven Daycare & Preschool was born from a simple wish: to give little children a safe, loving and fun place to learn. Today it is home to daycare kids from 6 months, preschoolers from age 2, and weekend playzone adventurers up to age 10.

Every child here is loved, cared for, and encouraged to learn through play. We believe every child deserves a happy childhood filled with love, laughter, discovery and learning.

  • Daycare (full day / half day) from 6 months
  • Preschool in two shifts, Sunday–Thursday
  • Birthdays and special days celebrated together

Next door: Saalim Wisdom School

Little Heaven shares its building with Saalim Wisdom School — our sister concern and English Medium school at Dhanmondi 4, House 12/B. As your little one grows, the journey continues in the same trusted place.

Admission fees at Little Heaven can also be adjusted later with Saalim Wisdom School, as announced on our official page.

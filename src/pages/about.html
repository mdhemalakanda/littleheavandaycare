Our Story

Serving parents since 2006

Kids Express School & Day-Care Centre began with one simple wish: to give the children of Mirpur a happy, safe place to learn. Nearly two decades later, we are still that same warm corner of Shewra Para — now trusted by hundreds of families.

We are a specialized English-version school for toddlers, pre-schoolers and kindergarten-goers, with a day-care service that works like a big family bond. In today's busy world, children need a place where they can share affection, care and love with one another — that is exactly what we built.

  • English-version curriculum from play group to pre-primary
  • Day-care service beside Metro pillar 308, Mirpur
  • Birthdays and celebrations — every child feels special

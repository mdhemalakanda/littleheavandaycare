Learning Activities

Real activities from our classrooms — every one backed by a post on our official pages.

Children learn best when learning feels like play. Here is what our days actually look like — straight from Little Heaven's official Facebook and Instagram.

Colour Matching

Our little learners match different colours while exploring and identifying them — building visual discrimination, hand-eye coordination, concentration and early problem-solving skills.

Problem-Solving Challenges

Hands-on activities where children explore, experiment, think independently and discover creative solutions — learning that trying again is part of learning.

Pretend Play & Shopping Day

Little shoppers count, choose and 'buy' in our pretend market — exploring money, practising communication and growing social skills.

Sensory Play

Rolling, touching and exploring different movements and textures — sensory play builds fine motor skills, coordination and creativity.

Water Play

Swimming-pool water play activities — splashing fun that builds confidence and body awareness.

Hygiene & Healthy Habits

Global Handwashing Day and everyday routines — small hands learn big habits that last a lifetime.

Karate & Movement

Karate classes at Saalim Wisdom School build strength, balance, focus, discipline and respect for others.

Each activity above is shown in a real post or reel on our official Facebook page — see the Videos page to watch them.

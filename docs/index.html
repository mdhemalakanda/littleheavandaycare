Welcome to Kids Express

A second home for your little one

At Kids Express School & Day-Care Centre, children learn through play, stories, songs and smiles. We are a specialized English-version pre-school — toddlers, pre-schoolers and kindergarten-goers grow here with love, care and attention, just like family.

Our day-care is not just a service — it is a warm family bond where children share affection, care and love with one another.

  • English-version curriculum from the very first class
  • Safe, homely day-care along Metro pillar 308, Mirpur
  • Play-based learning with individual attention

A safe, loving second home for your child — right here in Mirpur.

Day-Care Program

Kids Express day-care is a family, not a facility. While you work with peace of mind, your child plays, eats, rests and learns under the watchful eyes of caring teachers.

The school itself says it best: "A day-care is not just a day-care — it is a big family bond." Children here share affection, care and love with one another, every single day.

  • A gentle, homely daily routine
  • Caring teachers watching over every child
  • Birthdays and festival days celebrated together

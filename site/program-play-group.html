Play Group

First steps into school life — gentle, joyful and full of play.

Play Group

In Play Group, school feels like one big play date. Children learn colours, rhymes and simple words through toys, songs and gentle routines.

Separation worries melt quickly here. Our teachers welcome each child by name, and within weeks, tears at the door turn into smiles.

  • Rhymes, colours and shapes
  • Gentle good habits — hello, thank you, tidy up
  • English words through songs

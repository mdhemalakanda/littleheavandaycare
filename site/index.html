Welcome to Little Heaven

A little place with big dreams

At Little Heaven Daycare & Preschool, every child is loved, cared for and encouraged to learn through play. We focus on making every child's early learning journey enjoyable — through play-based learning, creative activities, project work, social interaction and caring guidance.

We are in the same building as Saalim Wisdom School in Dhanmondi 4 — so your child's learning journey can continue smoothly to primary school.

  • Play-based & child-friendly learning
  • Safe, comfortable and caring surroundings
  • Individual attention for every child
